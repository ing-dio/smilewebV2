    How SmileWeb works

    Privacy first

    Smile detection runs entirely in your browser with WebAssembly. Video frames are drawn to a canvas, measured, and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no cookies for tracking, no analytics.

    How the smile is detected

    A lightweight face-landmark model maps 478 points on your face. SmileWeb watches the mouth smile blendshapes, smooths them over time and only reacts once your smile is real and steady — so a cough or a frown never counts.

    Nice on slow devices

    The detector is loaded lazily, the joke library streams in chunks, and rendering falls back to a static preview whenever the GPU is busy or motion is reduced.

    Accessible by default

    Keyboard shortcuts, screen-reader announcements, high-contrast support and a reduced-motion mode are built in. You can always skip the camera and browse jokes instead.

    Before we borrow your camera

    SmileWeb needs a brief look through your webcam to see your smile. Here is exactly what happens:

    • Frames are analysed on your device and immediately discarded.
    • Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or shared. Ever.
    • You can stop the camera at any moment with one click.
